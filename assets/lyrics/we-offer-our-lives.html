Arnel Aquino, SJ

1. We offer our lives to the Lord up above,
We offer our hearts for it’s Jesus we love.
And now as we pray, may the Lord come and say
That we’ll share in His glory one day.

2. We offer our hopes for each day that we live,
We know without Christ we have nothing to give
And now as we pray, may the Lord come and say
That we’ll share in His glory one day.

Coda:
