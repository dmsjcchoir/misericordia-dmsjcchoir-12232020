World Youth Day ’95 Theme Song
Trina Belamide

1. For God so loved the world He gave us His Only Son, Jesus Christ our Savior, His most precious One. He has sent us His message of love, and sends those who hear To bring His message to everyone in a voice loud and clear.

2. Let us tell the world of his love, the greatest love the world has known Search the world for those who have walked astray, and lead them home. Fill the world’s darkest corners with His light from up above, Walk every step, every mile, every road And tell the world, tell the world of His love.
