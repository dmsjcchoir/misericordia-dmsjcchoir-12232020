1. O come, Divine Messiah, the world in silence waits the day.
When hope shall ring its triumph, and sadness flee away.

Refrain:
Sweet Savior haste; Come, come to earth;
Dispel the night and show Thy face,
And bid us hail the dawn of grace.

2. O thou whom nations sighed for, whome priests and prophets long foretold,
Will break the captive felters, redeem the long lost fold.
Repeat Refrain and Verse 1

3. Thou'll come in peace and meekness, and lowly will Thy cradle be
As veiled in human weakness, Thy majesty we shall see.
Repeat Refrain and Verse 1
