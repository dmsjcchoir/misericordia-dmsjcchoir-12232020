Randy Figuracion, SDB
Album & Scorebook: Misa Figuracion 1

1. O God, You are my God for You I long.
My soul is thirsting for the joy that comes from You alone.
My being like the earth is parched and lifeless.
Without You by my side I’ll lose my way.

Refrain:
In You, O Lord, is my strength and my salvation.
Only in You my soul shall find its rest
You are my song, my glory, the rock of my strength.
Your constant love is my refuge.

2. Your kindness is greater good than life.
My lips shall glorify Your name forever Lord.
And I will tell my friends of Your great love.
The goodness You have shown me from of old.
Repeat Refrain

3. I trust in You, O God, in my confusion.
My heart is deeply troubled filled with insecurities.
But You are there to lead me all the way.
You take me by the hand I’m not alone.
Repeat Refrain
