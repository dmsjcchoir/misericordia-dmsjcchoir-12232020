Norman Agatep

1. When my eyes are heavy with sorrow
When my body aches with grief
When my soul is filled with anguish
When my heart grows weak

2. You save me from my troubles
You deliver me from all shame
You rescue me from my enemies
When I call your name

Refrain:
Let all the earth resound in praise
Let the world with hearts ablaze
Sing the wonders of your love sent from above with one voice
Let the seas rejoice in your Word
Let the hills call out and be heard
Let the chorus fill the air with this pray'r
I sing to you a new song

3. O Lord, you are my fortress
O Lord, you are my home
And underneath your wing
You shelter me from the storm
Repeat Verse 2 and Refrain

Bridge:
For you have done great things
To surround me with your love
With a grateful heart I sing, I sing
Repeat Refrain

Coda:
I sing a song. I sing a song.
