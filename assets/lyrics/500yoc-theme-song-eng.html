Carlo Magno Marcelo
The Official Mission Song for the 500 Years of Christianity in the Phillipines

Refrain:
We stand before the grand horizon,
Five hundred years of faith, grateful today.
We bear the gift of mission.
Totally Yours, we give ourselves!
Faithfully Yours until the end!
To Your mission, Lord, we give our yes!

1. Go where the sun rises above the hills,
Share the word and serve those who are in need.
Let the Morning Star accompany our way.
Spread the fire of mission for the Lord.
Repeat Refrain

2. We are gifted to give and live our gift:
Mercy, compassion, justice, and peace.
The sign our world today will come to believe
Is the love we have for one and all!

Bridge:
We bear the gift of mission: Mercy, compassion, justice, and peace!
Repeat Refrain

Coda:
To Your mission, Lord, we give our yes!
