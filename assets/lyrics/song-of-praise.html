Randy Figuracion, SDB
Album & Scorebook: Misa Figuracion 1

Refrain:
How great are Your works, O Lord;
Your name is praised in all lands.
For Your goodness and love,
For Your greatness and pow’r.
Forever and ever we sing of Your praise.

1. Give thanks to the Lord, praise Him day by day,
From morning till noon, unto the night;
His praise we all proclaim.
Repeat Refrain

2. You give us strength, O Lord,
Your hand was there for us,
You turned our sadness into joy,
Your praise we now proclaim.
Repeat Refrain

2. Give thanks to the Lord, He covered us with His might,
And like the cedars we shall endure,
And live in the house of the Lord.
Repeat Refrain
