Alejandro Consolacion II

Refrain:
Ubi caritas et amor,
Deus ibi est, Deus ibi est.
Ubi caritas et amor,
Deus ibi est, Deus ibi est.

1. The love of Christ be upon us
Let us rejoice and be Glad in Him
Let us love and serve the Lord
And love each other from the depths of our hearts.
Repeat Refrain

2. Let us gather ourselves in His presence
Let's be one, not divided
Let's give peace to one another
And may the Lord be on our side.
Repeat Refrain

3. And in the company of the blessed
We see God's face in our glory
Christ's pure and abounded joy
Forever and ever.
Repeat Refrain
