Traditional

1. Immaculate Mother, to you do we plead,
To ask God, our Father, for help in our need.

Refrain:
Ave, Ave, Ave Maria! Ave, Ave, Ave Maria!

2. We pray for our country, the land of our birth,
We pray for all nations that peace be on earth.
Repeat Refrain

3. In grief and temptation, in joy and in pain,
We’ll seek thee, our Mother, nor seek thee in vain.
Repeat Refrain
