David Haas
Album & Scorebook: Prayers from the Upper Room & Sing of Him

1. I will come to you in the silence,
I will lift you from all your fear.
You will hear My voice, I claim you as My choice,
Be still and know I am here.

2. I am hope for all who are hopeless,
I am eyes for all who long to see.
In the shadows of the night, I will be your light.
Come and rest in Me.

Refrain:
Do not be afraid I am with you.
I have called you each by name.
Come and follow Me, I will bring you home;
I love you and you are Mine.

3. I am strength for all the despairing
Healing for the ones who dwell in shame.
All the blind will see, the lame will all run free,
And all will know My name.
Repeat Refrain

3. I am the Word that leads all to freedom.
I am the peace the world cannot give.
I will call your name, embracing all your pain,
Stand up, now walk, and live!
Repeat Refrain
