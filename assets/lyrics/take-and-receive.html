Prayer from the Spiritual Exercises of St. Ignatius of Loyola
Manoling Francisco, SJ
Album & Scorebook: The Best of Bukas Palad (vol. 2)

1. Take and receive, O Lord, my liberty,
Take all my will, my mind, my memory.
All things I hold, and all I own are Thine.
Thine was the gift, to Thee I all resign.

2. Do Thou direct, and govern all and sway,
Do what Thou wilt command and I obey.
Only Thy grace, Thy love on me bestow,
These make me rich, all else will I forego.
