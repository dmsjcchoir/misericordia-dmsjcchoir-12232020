Randy Figuracion, SDB
Album & Scorebook: Misa Figuracion 1

1. We bring to You the works of our hands,
This bread we give fruit of our toil,
Make of it the bread of salvation,
To change our hearts, our very lives.

Refrain:
Receive, we pray, O Lord. Our gift, our offering.
Take our hearts, take our lives, all we have is Yours,
Now we give to You our all.

2. We bring to You the works of our hands,
This wine we give fruit of the vine.
Make of it the cup of salvation
To change our ways, our very selves.
Repeat Refrain
